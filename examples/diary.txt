FISHRAND - THE FISH-GUARDED DIARY

(date: some very serious day)

The humans think their secrets are safe. And they are right -
because they are guarded by a goldfish named Fibonacci.

Today Fibonacci swam 147.3 cm according to the builder of this
apparatus. He zigzagged 9 times and looked at me with an unblinking
stare that I choose to interpret as cryptographic intent.

I hid spare fish flakes in the crook of the power strip. Operational
security demands refreshments.

Note to future self: the fish contributes nothing to the actual
security. The OS does. Do not tell the fish; it has a fragile ego but
a very real swim bladder.

-- me, trusting a fish with my secrets
